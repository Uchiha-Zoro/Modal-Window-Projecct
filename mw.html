I'm Roronoa Zoro, the Pirate Hunter ⚔☠⚔

Aspiring to become the world's greatest swordsman, Zoro wields the Three sword(Santoryu) style, fighting with three swords at once — one in each hand and one clenched between his teeth. Fiercely loyal to Luffy and the Straw Hat crew, he's sworn never to lose again after his defeat to Mihawk, carrying that promise as a debt he intends to repay in full.
